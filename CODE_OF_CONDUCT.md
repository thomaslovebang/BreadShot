# Community code of conduct

BreadShot should be a place where people can improve software without being belittled, harassed, or excluded.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient across differences in language and experience.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Give reproducible evidence and assume good faith while discussing failures.
- Protect the privacy of users, testers, and contributors.
- Accept maintainer decisions about product scope without turning disagreement into personal conflict.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, doxxing, publication of private material, sustained disruption, and personal attacks are not tolerated in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile contact or, when confidentiality is important, through [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/thomaslovebang/BreadShot/security/advisories/new) with the title `Community conduct report`.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock conversations, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban participation when necessary. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and fairly as practical. Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation.
